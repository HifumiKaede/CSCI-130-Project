Our Story

Overview

Rice and Vows is a premier catering and event management service based in Quezon City, dedicated to elevating the wedding experience through culinary excellence and seamless execution. We bridge the gap between traditional Filipino hospitality and contemporary event design.

Our History

Founded in 2026, Rice and Vows began as a boutique catering team with a passion for storytelling through food. We set out to create a service that prioritizes peace of mind alongside gourmet flavors, transforming the complexities of wedding planning into a curated journey of excellence.

Our Mission

To provide couples with a stress-free, high-end catering experience by combining meticulous planning, world-class cuisine, and heartfelt service.

Our Vision

To be the leading choice for wedding catering in the Philippines, recognized for our innovation in culinary arts and our unwavering commitment to perfection.
